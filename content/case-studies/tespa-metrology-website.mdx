## The challenge

Tespa Pvt Ltd manufactures and supplies metrology equipment — precision
measurement instruments sold mainly to industrial and manufacturing buyers.
Without a website, the only way for a prospective buyer to learn what Tespa
offered was a direct conversation, with no way to browse the range or share a
link beforehand.

For a technical, considered purchase like measurement instruments, buyers
expect to research specifications and options online before they ever pick up
the phone.

## What we built

We focused on making the product range easy to browse and easy to act on.

- **A structured product catalogue.** Metrology instruments are organised by
  category, so buyers can find the type of equipment they need quickly.
- **Detailed product pages.** Each product page presents specifications and
  imagery clearly, giving technical buyers the information they need to
  shortlist equipment.
- **A direct enquiry path.** Every product links straight to an enquiry flow,
  so interest turns into a conversation instead of a dead end.

## Why it matters for a technical buyer

Industrial buyers rarely make a snap decision on measurement equipment —
specifications, tolerances, and compatibility with existing processes all
have to check out before a purchase gets approved internally, often by
someone other than the person who first found the website. A structured
catalogue that can be browsed, shortlisted, and shared internally as a link
does far more selling than a single conversation ever could, because it lets
the buyer make their case to their own team without waiting on Tespa to
re-explain the range each time.

## The outcome

Tespa Pvt Ltd now has a website that does the work of a product catalogue and
a first point of contact combined — buyers can browse the range on their own
time and reach out when they're ready, instead of the company depending
entirely on word of mouth and direct outreach. It's also becoming the default
link the sales team shares with new leads instead of a PDF catalogue.
