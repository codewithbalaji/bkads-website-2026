## The challenge

Sri Balaji Printers runs a busy commercial print shop, but like many
established local businesses, it had no online presence at all. Customers who
found the shop did so through word of mouth or by walking past — there was no
way to see the range of print work on offer, or to reach out before showing
up in person.

## What we built

We built a site that puts the press's work front and centre and makes the
first step easy for a new customer.

- **A services overview.** The full range of print services is laid out
  clearly, so visitors immediately understand what the shop can do.
- **A gallery of print work.** Real examples of past jobs give new customers
  confidence in the quality on offer before they place an order.
- **A simple enquiry path.** Contact details and an enquiry form mean a
  customer can start a conversation about their print job online, any time.

## Why it matters for a local print shop

Print work is judged on quality, and quality is hard to sell over the phone.
Most customers choosing a printer want to see finished samples first —
business cards, packaging, signage — before they trust a shop with their own
order. Without a gallery, that proof only existed inside the shop itself,
which meant every new customer had to walk in cold to find out if the work
was any good. Putting that gallery online turns a first-time visitor into
someone who already trusts the quality before they ever make contact.

## The outcome

Sri Balaji Printers now has an online presence that matches the quality of
its work, giving new customers a way to discover the shop and start a
conversation before they ever walk through the door. The gallery also gives
the business something to point to when quoting new, larger clients.
