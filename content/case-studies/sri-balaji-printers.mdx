## The challenge

Sri Balaji Printers runs a busy commercial print shop where dozens of jobs move
through design, plating, printing, and finishing every week. The information that
kept those jobs on track was spread across paper job cards, WhatsApp threads, and
a handful of spreadsheets that only one or two people fully understood.

When a customer called to ask where their order was, answering meant walking the
floor and asking around. Nothing was wrong with the craft — the gap was purely in
**visibility**.

## What we built

We worked closely with the team to map how a job actually flows through the shop,
then built a focused web portal around that reality rather than forcing a generic
tool onto it.

- **One order, entered once.** The front desk captures customer, specification,
  and deadline at intake — no re-keying later.
- **Clear production stages.** Each job moves through named steps, so its status
  is obvious at a glance instead of buried in a message thread.
- **A shared board.** Everyone from the counter to the press floor sees the same
  live picture of what's in progress and what's due.

We kept the interface deliberately plain and fast, because it had to work for
people standing at a machine, not just at a desk.

## The outcome

The shop now runs from a single source of truth. Handovers between stages are
smoother, fewer details slip between paper and press, and answering "where's my
order?" no longer means a walk across the floor.

> We wanted the team to spend their attention on print quality, not on chasing
> paperwork — the portal quietly takes care of the tracking.

As real usage data builds up, the same foundation is ready to surface reporting
on turnaround and workload — a natural next step rather than a rebuild.
