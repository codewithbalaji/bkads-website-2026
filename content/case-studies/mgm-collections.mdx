## The challenge

MGM Collections sells gold-covering and fancy jewellery, and a large share of its
orders already moved through WhatsApp — customers would browse photos, ask
questions, and confirm an order over chat. Any new website risked breaking that
familiar flow if it forced people through an unrelated checkout.

Behind the scenes, the picture was similarly fragmented: stock, orders, and
billing lived across notebooks and spreadsheets, with no single place to see
what had actually been sold.

## What we built

We designed the platform around the ordering habit customers already had,
rather than replacing it.

- **A full storefront with cart.** A React.js catalogue with categories,
  product detail pages, and a proper cart — so browsing feels like a normal
  online store.
- **WhatsApp checkout, built in.** Customers can move straight from cart to a
  pre-filled WhatsApp order message, keeping the conversation the team is
  used to handling.
- **An admin panel.** Staff manage the catalogue, prices, and incoming orders
  from one dashboard instead of editing files by hand.
- **Billing software.** Order and billing data live in the same system as the
  storefront, powered by Firebase, so there's one source of truth instead of
  three.

## The outcome

MGM Collections now has an online storefront that feels native to how its
customers already shop, backed by an admin panel and billing system that
replace the old spreadsheet-and-notebook routine.
