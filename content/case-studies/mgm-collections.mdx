## The challenge

MGM Collections had built a loyal following on the strength of its products and
word of mouth. But when a new customer went looking for them online, there was
little to find — no easy way to browse the range, understand what was on offer,
or get in touch outside of shop hours.

In a market where the first impression is almost always digital, the brand's
online presence didn't yet match the quality of what it sold.

## What we built

The goal was a website that felt as considered as the collection itself: quick to
load, effortless on a phone, and built around the products.

- **An image-led catalogue.** Products are organised so visitors can browse by
  what they're actually looking for, with photography given room to breathe.
- **Mobile-first performance.** The vast majority of visitors arrive on a phone,
  so the site was tuned to feel instant on mobile connections.
- **Frictionless enquiries.** Clear calls to action turn "I like this" into a
  conversation, without a clunky checkout getting in the way.

We kept the design clean and brand-focused, letting the products carry the page.

## The outcome

MGM Collections now has an online home that reflects the brand — one customers can
explore at any hour and share with a single link. The path from browsing to
enquiry is short and obvious, giving the team warmer conversations to pick up.

The site is structured so a full transactional storefront can be layered on later
without starting over, keeping the door open as the business grows online.
