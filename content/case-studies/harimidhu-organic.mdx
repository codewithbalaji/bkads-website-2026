## The challenge

Harimidhu Organic sells premium organic products, where trust in quality and
sourcing matters as much as the product itself. The brand needed a storefront
that looked and felt as considered as what it sells — but without creating a
system only a developer could keep running.

## What we built

We built the storefront and the back office as one connected system from day
one, rather than bolting on management tools later.

- **A premium storefront.** A React.js catalogue built to showcase organic
  products clearly, with categories, product detail pages, and a clean
  checkout flow.
- **Firebase-backed data.** Products, orders, and stock are stored centrally,
  so the storefront always reflects what's actually available.
- **A store management system.** Staff can add products, update stock and
  pricing, and review orders through an admin panel — no code changes needed
  for everyday running of the shop.

## The outcome

Harimidhu Organic now has an online store that matches the quality of its
products, with a management system that puts day-to-day control in the
team's own hands instead of a developer's queue.
