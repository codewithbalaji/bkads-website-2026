## The challenge

Revive Wardrobe sells sustainable fashion out of Dubai, which meant the store
had to handle real-world logistics from day one — international payments,
shipping across regions, and fulfilment handled by a third-party logistics
partner rather than the brand itself.

A generic storefront template wasn't going to cut it; the checkout and
fulfilment flow needed to talk directly to outside systems.

## What we built

We treated the integrations as the core of the project, not an afterthought
bolted on at the end.

- **A full storefront.** A fashion-focused e-commerce site with product
  catalogue, collections, and a conversion-ready checkout.
- **Payment gateway integration.** Checkout connects directly to a payment
  gateway suited to the brand's region and customer base.
- **Third-party logistics integration.** Orders flow straight into the
  logistics partner's system for fulfilment, instead of being manually
  re-entered order by order.

## The outcome

Revive Wardrobe now has a store where a customer's payment and shipment are
handled by connected systems end-to-end, freeing the team from manually
bridging the gap between "order placed" and "order shipped."
