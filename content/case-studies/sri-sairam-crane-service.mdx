## The challenge

Sri Sai Ram Crane Service runs a crane rental business in Chennai, where most
new enquiries had come through word of mouth and phone calls. There was no
website to show the fleet, share updates, or let a customer reach out outside
business hours.

## What we built

We built a site that does double duty — proof of capability for new
customers, and a direct channel for enquiries.

- **Fleet management.** A dedicated section presents the crane fleet clearly,
  so prospective customers can see capacity and capability at a glance.
- **An integrated blog.** A content section lets the business publish updates
  and build search visibility over time, rather than relying only on
  word of mouth.
- **Mail & WhatsApp integrations.** Enquiry forms route straight to email and
  WhatsApp, so a visitor can get in touch the same way they'd expect to call.

## The outcome

Sri Sai Ram Crane Service now has a website that represents the business
properly online, with fleet details, ongoing content, and direct enquiry
channels working together instead of leaving visitors with no way to follow
up.
