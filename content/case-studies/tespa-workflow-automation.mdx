## The challenge

Like many manufacturing businesses, Tespa Pvt Ltd relied on a steady flow of
documents — the kind of paperwork that has to be read, understood, and typed into
another system before work can move forward.

It was necessary work, but it was also **repetitive**. Capable people were spending
meaningful chunks of their week on manual reading and data entry, where fatigue
quietly turns into small mistakes that cost time to unwind later.

## What we built

Rather than replace the team's judgement, we automated the mechanical part around
it, keeping a person in the loop where it mattered.

- **Automated reading.** Incoming documents are processed by an AI step that
  pulls out the fields the team actually cares about.
- **Straight into the right place.** Extracted details are routed to the correct
  destination instead of being re-typed by hand.
- **A human check, not a human keyboard.** People review and approve rather than
  transcribe — faster, and less draining.

We rolled it out gradually on the highest-volume document types first, so the team
could build trust in the automation before widening its scope.

## The outcome

The most repetitive part of the day now largely takes care of itself. Staff spend
their attention on decisions and exceptions rather than routine entry, and the
process is more consistent because the machine never gets tired.

> The best automation feels invisible — the work just stops piling up.

With the foundation in place, additional document types and workflows can be added
to the same pipeline as the team finds new places worth automating.
